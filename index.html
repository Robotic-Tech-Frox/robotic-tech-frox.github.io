Who we are

We are Robotic Tech Frox, a FIRST® Tech Challenge team from Germany. From the very beginning, our mission has been to promote STEM values and inspire children to explore a passion for technology.

We are dedicated to continuous growth, always seeking new knowledge, skills and perspectives to enhance our expertise. Beyond our own development, we share our projects and discoveries with the community — nurturing a culture of learning, collaboration and mutual support.

We are 2× German Champion — and we keep going. By sharing knowledge, we aim to empower others, spark children's interest in STEM, and make a positive impact on our community and the wider world.

Our mission

Grow and excel in STEM and the FIRST® Tech Challenge by constantly improving our skills and knowledge — guided by Gracious Professionalism and Coopertition.

Every year we aim to do better than the last, learning and evolving together. We want every child — including students with disabilities — to have the chance to explore and enjoy STEAM education.
